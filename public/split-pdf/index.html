    How to split a PDF

    1. Add your PDF. Drop it on the page, or tap Select PDF.
    2. Choose how to split. Pick pages for one new PDF, or turn every page into its own PDF.
    3. Pick the pages. Tap them, or type numbers like 1-3, 8. The order you type is the order you get.
    4. Split and download. Get a single PDF, or a ZIP with one PDF per page.

    Questions and answers

    Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

    No. Nearfile opens the PDF inside this browser tab and does all the work there. Nothing is sent to a server, and closing the tab clears everything. Once the page has loaded, it even works offline.

    How do I type page ranges?

    Separate pages with commas and use a dash for ranges: 1-3, 5, 9-12. A range with a missing end runs to the edge of the document, so 8- means page 8 to the last page and -3 means pages 1 to 3.

    Can I change the page order?

    Yes. Pages are extracted in the order you type them, so 5, 1-4 puts page 5 first, and 10-1 reverses the first ten pages.

    Does splitting reduce quality?

    No. Pages are copied exactly as they are, so text stays selectable and images keep their resolution.

    Can I use a password-protected PDF?

    Not yet. If a PDF needs a password to open, or is locked against editing, open it in your PDF reader, save a copy without the protection, and use that copy.
