Convert, merge and compress files without uploading them

Nearfile compresses, merges and splits PDFs, turns images into PDFs, and resizes photos inside this browser tab. No sign-up, no waiting for uploads, and no copy of your files on anyone’s server.

Tools

  • PDF to image

    To JPG or PNG, one file or a ZIP

  • Merge PDF

    Any number of PDFs

Your files stay on this device

Most online converters upload your file, process it on their servers and send it back. Nearfile works the other way round: the conversion code arrives with the page, and your files go straight from your device into this tab.

So there’s nothing to upload, no queue and no account, and no copy of your documents sits on someone else’s computer. Close the tab and everything is gone.

How Nearfile handles privacy

Try it offline

  1. Open any tool and let the page finish loading.
  2. Turn off Wi‑Fi, or switch on airplane mode.
  3. Convert a file. It still works, because nothing needs to be sent anywhere.
