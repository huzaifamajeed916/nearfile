    How to resize images

    1. Add your images. Drop up to 30 JPG, PNG or WebP files on the page, or tap Select images.
    2. Choose the size. Scale by a percentage, or give a width, a height or both in pixels. Each picture shows the size it will become.
    3. Pick a format. Keep each image’s own format, or save them all as JPG, PNG or WebP.
    4. Resize and download. Save images one at a time, or all of them in a ZIP.

    Questions and answers

    Are my images uploaded anywhere?

    No. Nearfile resizes them inside this browser tab. Nothing is sent to a server, and closing the tab clears everything. Once the page has loaded, it even works offline.

    What does “keep proportions” do?

    The width and height stay in the same ratio, so nothing looks stretched. Give a width and the height follows, or give both and each image is scaled to fit inside that box. Turn it off to stretch images to exactly the size you type.

    Will making an image bigger make it sharper?

    No. Enlarging can’t add detail that was never captured, so the picture just gets softer. It’s best to resize down, which is why small images are left alone by default.

    Which format should I choose?

    JPG for photos, PNG for screenshots, logos and anything with transparency, WebP when you want the smallest files and the places you’ll use them support it. Turning a PNG with transparency into a JPG fills the see-through areas with white.

    Why did my file get bigger?

    More pixels or a higher quality setting means a bigger file. Lower the percentage or the quality, or save as JPG or WebP instead of PNG.

    What happens to location and camera details?

    Resized images are saved without hidden details such as GPS location, camera model and the date taken. Photos are turned the right way up first, so nothing appears sideways.

    Is there a size limit?

    You can add up to 30 images at a time, and each result can be up to 40 megapixels (about 7,000 × 5,700 pixels). Very large images may be too much for a phone’s memory.
