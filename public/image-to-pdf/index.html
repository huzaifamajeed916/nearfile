    How to convert images to PDF

    1. Add your images. Drop up to 20 JPG, PNG or WebP files anywhere on the page, or tap Select images.
    2. Put them in order. Drag the thumbnails or use the arrows. Image 1 becomes page 1.
    3. Set up the pages. Choose A4, Letter or a page that fits each image, then the orientation and margins.
    4. Convert and download. Get one combined PDF, or a separate PDF for each image in a ZIP.

    Questions and answers

    Are my images uploaded to a server?

    No. Nearfile reads them directly from your device and builds the PDF inside this browser tab. Nothing is sent anywhere, and closing the tab clears everything. Once the page has loaded, it even works with your internet connection switched off.

    Will my images lose quality?

    JPG and PNG images go into the PDF exactly as they are, without being compressed again. WebP images, and phone photos stored sideways with a rotation tag, are redrawn once as high-quality JPG so they appear the right way up.

    Which page size should I choose?

    A4 is the standard paper size in most of the world, and Letter is used in the US and Canada. Fit to image makes each page exactly the size of its image, so there are no white borders unless you add a margin.

    How many images can I convert at once?

    Up to 20. There’s no file size limit, but big batches need more memory, so older phones may do better with fewer, smaller images.

    Can I convert iPhone HEIC photos?

    Yes, if you choose them with the Select images button: iPhone and iPad hand photos to the browser as JPG. HEIC files copied to a computer need converting to JPG first.

    Does it work on my phone?

    Yes. It works in current versions of Safari, Chrome, Firefox and Edge on phones, tablets and computers. On a touchscreen, press and hold a thumbnail for a moment, then drag it.
