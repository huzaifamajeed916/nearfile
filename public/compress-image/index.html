    How to compress images

    1. Add your images. Drop up to 30 JPG, PNG or WebP files on the page, or tap Select images.
    2. Set the quality. Move the slider: the sizes update as you go. Resize or change format for even smaller files.
    3. Check the result. Tap a picture to switch between the original and the compressed version.
    4. Download. Save images one at a time, or all of them in a ZIP.

    Questions and answers

    Are my images uploaded anywhere?

    No. Nearfile compresses them inside this browser tab. Nothing is sent to a server, and closing the tab clears everything. Once the page has loaded, it even works offline.

    What quality should I use?

    For photos, 70 to 80% usually looks the same as the original and often cuts the file size by half or more. Below about 50%, blocky patches start to show. Tap a picture to compare before you download.

    Why didn’t my PNG get smaller?

    PNG is a lossless format, so the quality slider can’t shrink it. Save it as JPG or WebP, or make it smaller with Longest side. If re-saving wouldn’t make an image smaller, you keep the original file.

    Should I use WebP?

    WebP files are usually smaller than JPGs of the same quality, and every current browser and most apps open them. Some older software can’t, so choose JPG if you’re unsure.

    What happens to location and camera details?

    Compressed images are saved without hidden details such as GPS location, camera model and the date taken. Photos are turned the right way up first, so nothing appears sideways.

    Does resizing help?

    A lot. Phone photos are often around 4000 pixels wide, while 1920 pixels is plenty for screens, email and social media. Resizing to that often makes files four times smaller.
