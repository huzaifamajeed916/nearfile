    How to convert a PDF to images

    1. Add your PDF. Drop it on the page, or tap Select PDF.
    2. Choose the pages. Every page starts selected. Tap a page to leave it out, or type page numbers.
    3. Pick a format and resolution. JPG for small files, PNG for sharp text. 300 DPI for printing.
    4. Convert and download. Save each image on its own, or all of them in one ZIP.

    Questions and answers

    Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

    No. Nearfile opens the PDF inside this browser tab and does all the work there. Nothing is sent to a server, and closing the tab clears everything. Once the page has loaded, it even works offline.

    Should I choose JPG or PNG?

    JPG makes much smaller files and suits photos and scanned pages. PNG is lossless, so text, charts and screenshots stay perfectly sharp, but the files are bigger.

    Which resolution should I pick?

    72 DPI matches the page’s size on screen and makes small files. 150 DPI is a good all-rounder for sharing. 300 DPI is print quality, with much larger files. Very large pages, such as posters, may be drawn a little smaller to stay within your device’s limits.

    How do I convert only some pages?

    Tap the pages you don’t want, or type in the Pages box, for example 1-3, 8, 10-. A range ending in a dash, like 10-, runs to the last page.

    Can I use a password-protected PDF?

    Not yet. If a PDF needs a password to open, or is locked against editing, open it in your PDF reader, save a copy without the protection, and use that copy.

    Why does a font look slightly different?

    A few PDFs don’t include their fonts. When that happens, your browser uses the closest match it has, as most PDF readers do.
